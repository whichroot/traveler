  classical = XOR of random 14-bit words
  syndrome  = (S_1, S_3) over GF(2^7) = BinField<7, 0x83>
======================================================================

  weight |      patterns |  classical invisible |  syndrome invisible
  -------+---------------+----------------------+--------------------
       1 |           120 |                    0 |                   0
       2 |          7140 |                    1 |                   0
       3 |        280840 |                   19 |                   0
       4 |       8214570 |                  454 |                   0
       5 |     190578024 |                11620 |               12006

  Rows 1-4: the BCH bound (designed distance 2T+1 = 5) forbids an
  invisible difference of weight <= 4. Zero, over every pattern.
  Row 5: the bound stops. The syndrome key collides too -- the
  theorem is TIGHT, not conservative.
